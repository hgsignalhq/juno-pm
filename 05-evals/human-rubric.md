# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno Signal Triage: Live session log ingestion, concept friction synthesis, and unaddressed student question triage queue.

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno Signal Triage: Live session log ingestion, concept friction synthesis, and unaddressed student question triage queue.
- **Value proposition:** Accurately identifies real classroom conceptual friction points and drafts context-grounded, ready-to-send replies for unanswered student questions within 60 seconds of class ending, saving the instructor 20+ minutes of manual log searching while maintaining zero hallucinated quotes.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Factual Grounding (deal-breaker) | Are 100% of the extracted student quotes, names, and timestamps verbatim substring matches from the raw session logs? | Yes / No | Any quote is fabricated, altered, truncated misleadingly, or attributed to the wrong student. | n/a | Every quote, student name, and timestamp in the Concept Friction table and Unaddressed Action Queue matches the raw Zoom or Slack export exactly. |
| Concept Friction Accuracy | How accurate and pedagogically useful is the concept friction clustering and severity tagging? | 1 to 5 | Hallucinates friction topics, maps questions to the wrong curriculum module, or tags routine logistics (e.g., "can you hear me?") as High Severity friction. | Correctly groups questions under the right syllabus module, but misses secondary nuances or tags severity arbitrarily without clear friction signal. | Accurately groups related student confusion under curriculum learning objectives, assigns High/Med/Low severity based on frequency and conceptual depth, and provides a clear, actionable recommended re-explanation. |
| Response Actionability | How ready are the pre-drafted instructor responses for the Unaddressed Action Queue? | 1 to 5 | Generic boilerplate ("Great question, check the syllabus"), technically incorrect, or fails to address what the student actually asked. | Factually correct and answers the question, but requires substantial instructor editing for tone, conciseness, or specific classroom context before sending. | Precise, pedagogical, and concise explanation citing the relevant slide or lab context; the instructor can review and click send without making edits. |
| Triage Recall | Did Juno capture all unaddressed student questions from the session logs? | 1 to 5 | Misses more than 40% of unanswered student questions, or clutters the queue with questions already answered live on the transcript. | Captures major unanswered questions, but misses an edge-case question in a fast thread or includes 1–2 items that were already addressed live. | 100% recall of genuine unanswered student queries, correctly excludes questions answered live during class, and flags questions waiting >10 minutes as High-Priority. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** We will score all post session replies and 100% of the data since it is low volume
- **Who scores them with you:** Me + other teachers on their own data when we share Juno with them for testing
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** we calibrate when the disagree rate is higher than 10%

## Pass bar

Pass Question 1 (Factual Grounding = Yes), plus an average score of >= 4.0 across Questions 2, 3, and 4, with no individual question scoring below 3.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Factual Grounding: any "no"
- 1. Hallucinating student sentiment or engagement without quoting source messages. 2. Attempting to message or email students directly without instructor review. 3. Execution exceeding the 45-second timeout or returning malformed JSON.
